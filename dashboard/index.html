Drive

Ready

Hold a direction to keep driving. Release, Space or Esc stops. Keys: WASD or arrows.

Speed limit

Robot: --

Scales everything: arrows, Raw PWM, line following, barcode moves and obstacle avoidance. Line following runs at its own Speed × this limit; if the car stalls on the line, raise that Speed. Resets to 40% on reboot.

Line following

Robot: --

Speed is straight-line power. Curve power is added to the outer wheel and taken from the inner one. −/+ applies instantly, even while following. Defaults 35 / 27, reset on reboot.

Raw PWM

Firmware stops raw drive after 1 s without a fresh command.

Motor trim

Robot: --

Lower the faster motor. −/+ applies instantly. Affects Raw PWM, arrows and line following. Resets on reboot.
